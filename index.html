SEC Risk Shift Tracker

What changed in a company's risk narrative between filings?

This pipeline reads SEC 10-K disclosures, compares year-over-year language with FinBERT embeddings, and makes source passages retrievable for cited questions. The public repository includes processing modules and derived metrics.

2024

Signals of bank distress

An investigation into how market volatility, financial ratios, and macroeconomic conditions relate to bank distress.

The study compared classification approaches across a documented dataset of 26 public U.S. banks. It explored modified Altman Z-score trends alongside price and economic features. Results are presented as findings from the project, not a deployed risk model.
